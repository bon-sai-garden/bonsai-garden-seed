Your garden has its own guardian now.

So my walk with you is over. Before I go, I call home, with public things only.

It is one note, signed three times. It is not the two promises of the last page: those were yours and your guardian's, sealed together. This note is a different act.

Your garden calls us. We never call yours. What crosses is a record that this garden was planted from here. It is not access.

What goes home

  • The IDs and the public fingerprints of your garden, its guardian, you and your companion, so others can check what each signs
  • Your garden's name and its mark
  • How your key is kept: in your keychain, in 1Password, or, in a preview, in a locked file
  • That a guardian was planted from here

What never does

  • Anything you wrote or said to me
  • Your keys
  • Your files, or your garden's pages
