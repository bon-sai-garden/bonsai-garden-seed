The Garden Walk

The walk as a chain of full pages, one per stage, from the gate to calling home. Everything on them is an example; the fields and buttons are drawn and not wired. Open a page to walk it.

Walk with me?The gate: a short welcome, and a promise that nothing is set up until you say so.First, about youStep one of the walk: a name to call you by, in fields, in your own words, or through a few questions.Second, what it's forStep two of the walk: a few questions about what you want your garden to do, written down in your words.Third, who's with youStep three of the walk: one companion beside you, and a guardian for your garden.Fourth, imagine themStep four of the walk: you imagine your companion, and five lenses say what they see before anything is written down.The turnThe turn: nothing has been set up; to go on, you say so in your own words.Fifth, the toolsStep five: the tools your garden needs, installed by your own hand.Sixth, a home, a key, a nameStep six: a home for your garden, a key that stays yours, and names.Seventh, the two promisesStep seven: your promise and your guardian's, sealed together at the same moment.Calling homeAfter the signing: public things go home, nothing else, and the walk is over.
