First, about you

I'd like to know a little about you.

You can fill in a few short fields, write it your own way, or I can ask you a few questions: four is plenty, six at most.

Whichever you like. A name to call you by is enough.

This is a walk. I'll show you how it works, and nothing is set up until you tell me you want it.

Pick one way in

I won't guess them.
Pointing me to a link isn't asking me to read it. I'll ask once whether you'd like me to look, or just keep it.
Kept exactly as it is, beside what you write.
Question 1 · four is plenty

What should I call you?

…and, if you'd like to say, which pronouns I should use.

What I might ask, in order
  1. What should I call you?
  2. What fills most of your days right now?
  3. When someone helps you well, what do they do?
  4. What would you like me to know about you from the start?
  5. If you're still with me: Is there anything you'd like me never to assume about you?
  6. And: Is there anywhere I could look to know you a little better?
