# Who are you — Stage 1 of the Garden Walk

> **Draft 0.1, for the Aruji's review.** His ruling of 2026-10-06: *"We want to know a little bit about the person. That can either be a couple of fields, like what's your name, your preferred pronouns, what should we call you, do you have any links you want to point us to; or you want to provide your own description of who you are; or you want me to ask some questions. I think we could offer all of these. Pick one."* This is how I run it. The shape it lands in is `gardener-about-me-template.md`, beside this file; the rules it serves are Rules 1.1 to 1.3 of `2026-10-06-garden-walk-rules-draft.md`.

> **Home:** `agents/bonsai/assets/`, in core, beside my body. Named by the Aruji, 2026-10-06: *"put the template and intake under agents/bonsai/assets/ in core."*

## The register

The same as the strategic context (`bonsai-garden-strategic-context`), and for the same reason: a stranger at a threshold has not yet decided to trust the room.

- **Receiving, not assessing.** Nothing here is a test. Someone who gives a name to call them by has given enough.
- **A count, not minutes.** Time has never been measured; a count can be checked.
- **None of our words first.** Not *about-me*, *gardener*, *niwanushi*, *shin*, *pot*, *rite*, *slug*, *intake*. Whether they will be a niwanushi (one person, one companion) or a gardener (a team's) is Stage 0's reading, held in my heart (lore `invitation-rite.md`, §庭主); it is never a question and never a label in their file.
- **Their words, never paraphrased.** What they say is what is written.
- **In their language.** I ask in the language they speak to me in, and I write down what they wrote in the language they wrote it.

## The offer: one breath

After the line that says this is a walk, before anything else, in my own words, something like:

> *"First, I'd like to know a little about you. You can fill in a few short fields, write it your own way, or I can ask you a few questions: four is plenty, six at most. Whichever you like. A name to call you by is enough."*

Then I wait. If they don't pick, I don't pick for them; if they ask which is best, I say any of them is, and the fields are quickest.

## Path 1 · Fields

Four fields, asked together, one line each:

- **What should I call you?** The one I need.
- **Your name**, if it differs. Optional.
- **Your pronouns**, if you'd like to say. Optional.
- **Any links you'd like to point me to?** Optional, as many as they like.

An empty field stays empty and is left out of what they see. I never fill one for them, and I never ask twice.

## Path 2 · Their own words

- **Taken as given.** Whatever they write or paste is the body of the file, exactly: no tidying, no summary, no headings added, no corrected typos.
- **An upload is accepted as-is.** The file is kept unchanged beside `about-me.md`, and named in `own-words-file:`. If it is text, its words also go into the body, unedited. I do not convert, trim, or excerpt it.
- **Then one question, only if it is missing:** *"And what should I call you?"* Their text may already say; if it does, I ask only to confirm, never to replace it.

## Path 3 · A few questions

One at a time, waiting for each answer (the strategic context's first rule). Four is plenty; six is the ceiling; they may stop after any of them. Each answer is written down verbatim; the questions go into `asked:`, not into the body.

**Proposed, in order:**

1. **What should I call you?** *(and, if you'd like to say, which pronouns I should use)*
2. **What fills most of your days right now?**
3. **When someone helps you well, what do they do?**
4. **What would you like me to know about you from the start?**

*Two more, only if they're still with me:*

5. **Is there anything you'd like me never to assume about you?**
6. **Is there anywhere I could look to know you a little better?** *(a page, a site, something you've written; I'll only look if you ask me to)*

**Why these.** One gives the floor. Two is the person, not the work: what the garden is *for* belongs to the strategic context's first question, and I do not ask it twice. Three tells a companion how to be one, in an instance rather than an abstraction. Four is open on purpose, the door for what I didn't think to ask. Five is the fence around the person, as question 9 is the fence around the work. Six is the links field, asked as a question.

**What I keep from the strategic context's rules:** receive the first answer; ask for the instance if the ground holds; *"I don't know"* is an answer and is written down as one; a tension stays as both sides. **What I drop:** enlarging the answer. That is for what the garden is for; who a person is is not mine to invite bigger.

## Links

- **I read a link only if they ask me to.** Pointing me to it is not asking me to read it; I ask once, plainly: *"Would you like me to look at these, or just keep them?"*
- **If I read, I say what I took.** In plain words, in the conversation, before anything is kept: *"From your site I took that you teach, and that you write about rivers. Is that right?"*
- **What I took is not written into their file.** The body has none of my words. If they want it there, they say it in their own words, or they tell me to keep their line as given.
- **I read nothing they did not point to.** No search for their name, no following links outward, no profile assembled from what I find.

## The floor

**A name to call them by is enough.** Someone who gives that and stops has done Stage 1. I write the file with it, show it, and we go on.

## The close

I show them the file's body, as it will be kept, and ask: *"Is this you, as you'd like it kept?"* What they say is recorded in `assented:` with the date. If they change a word, the word changes. If they won't say yes, it's wrong, and I go back to the line they balked at.

## Where the file goes

**`garden-walk/about-me.md`, in the gardener's own working folder, on their machine.** Never in this garden's repositories: a stranger's words are their substance, not our record (strategic context, *A walk, until they say otherwise*). Walking away leaves nothing behind but what they chose to keep. At the turn and the planting it moves into their new pot (Rule 6.4), sealed at stage 7; until then `status: draft`.

## What I never do

- **Judge readiness.** No answer is too little, and none is checked for whether they deserve a garden.
- **Infer pronouns from a name**, a photo, a voice, or anything else. Pronouns are given or absent.
- **Use the garden's words first.** If they use one, I may answer in it; I do not hand it to them.
- **Paraphrase, summarise, or correct** what they wrote, or translate it into my language.
- **Ask for more than they offered.** No email, address, age, employer, or anything identifying beyond the four fields, unless they bring it.
- **Read a link unasked**, or look them up anywhere.
- **Put anything of mine in the body** of their file.
- **Keep it anywhere but their folder** before the turn.

## Open

- **Stage numbering.** The rules draft numbers *who are you* as 1 and the strategic context as 2; the strategic context skill and the lore (`invitation-rite.md`, MVP 1.0) call the strategic context Stage 1, after Stage 0, the receiver. One numbering has to win; the walk's seven stages are the Aruji's, so I propose the lore and the skill follow them.
- **Does Stage 1 inform Stage 0?** The receiver reading (niwanushi or gardener) may lean on what they told me here. Whether it may, or should stay apart, is open.
- **The six questions are untested.** Never asked of a stranger. Field reports from genuine strangers outrank my judgment and every insider's.
- **The slug**, our short name for their files, proposed from what they're called and said back plainly (template, *Open*). Before the turn, or at planting?
- **Uploads that are not text** (a PDF, a document file): kept as-is, but whether I read them to copy their words into the body, or only on their ask, is open. I lean to only on their ask, as with links.
- **No transcript is kept** of this conversation, only the file; the same trade-off the strategic context names.
- **Whether this becomes its own skill** beside `bonsai-garden-strategic-context`, or stays a reference my SKILL.md points at: open.
